The coastline

Five beaches, five moods

Scroll sideways — each stretch of sand here has its own character, from watersports to surf breaks to quiet ferry crossings.

Panambur Beach

The city's most popular stretch — jet-skiing, banana boats and camel rides, plus a twice-yearly international kite festival.

Tannirbhavi Beach

Reached by a short ferry across the Gurupura River. Quieter, less commercial, good for an evening away from the crowds.

Someshwar Beach

Known for the dramatic Rudra Shile rock formations and sunset views, next to the old Someshwara temple.

Sasihithlu Beach

Where the Pavanje and Shambhavi rivers meet the sea — a popular spot for surfing.

Surathkal Beach

Near the NITK campus, with a working lighthouse and a long, scenic coastline.

Faith & heritage

Temples, chapels and shrines

Mangaluru's places of worship trace its layered history as clearly as any museum would.

Kadri Manjunatha Temple

Set at the base of the Kadri Hills, this is among the oldest Shiva temples in the region, with bronze idols and a serene tank-side setting.

Temple

Mangaladevi Temple

The temple the city takes its name from, dedicated to the goddess Mangaladevi — a good starting point for understanding local legend.

Temple

Kudroli Gokarnath Temple

A relatively young but striking temple in Dravidian style, known for intricate carvings, marble flooring and its role in the social reform movement led by Narayana Guru.

Temple

Polali Rajarajeshwari Temple

An old temple on the city's outskirts, tied to local history and folklore around the wider Tulu Nadu region.

Temple

St. Aloysius Chapel

Inside St. Aloysius College, the chapel's interior is covered in richly painted frescoes by the Italian Jesuit artist Antonio Moscheni — often compared to the Sistine Chapel in miniature.

Church

Rosario Cathedral

One of the city's oldest churches, with roots going back to the early Portuguese-era Christian community on this coast.

Green space & day trips

Parks, wildlife and nearby escapes

When you've had enough of temples and traffic, this is where locals go instead.

Pilikula Nisargadhama

A large eco-park on the city's edge with a zoo, botanical garden, science centre and boating lake — a full day out for families.

Kadri Park & Sammilan Shetty's Butterfly Park

A calm green pocket near the Kadri temple complex, and a dedicated butterfly park nearby for a slower-paced walk.

St. Mary's Islands

A cluster of small islands off the coast near Malpe, famous for their unusual columnar basalt rock formations — reachable by boat.

Bekal Fort

The largest fort in Kerala, roughly 57 km south of the city — a popular half-day trip along the coast.
